See it

A quick look around

Layers, pictures and video

Stack drawings like sheets of paper. Bring in a picture or a clip and choose what it is for.

  • Trace under my drawing: locked, faded, never exported
  • Part of the animation: included in your export
  • Hide, lock, rename, reorder, merge and set opacity per layer

Guides you can draw over, or keep

Pick a head, a body, a hand or a walk cycle. Turn it, change the perspective, then draw over it. Don’t want to draw over it? Paste guide turns it into real lines you can keep as the drawing itself.

Scenes, camera and sound

Split your film into named scenes, add pan, zoom and rotate with smooth, linear or hold easing, and put a sound track under your frames to time the action.

Export anywhere

Video (MP4 or WebM) with presets for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X, animated GIF, PNG frames or sequence, and a Brush X project file you can back up or share.

Made for phones too

Finger drawing, big touch targets, a side drawer for layers and settings, and an Android app you can install and test today.

An interactive guide for beginners

The first time you open Brush X, a step-by-step guide points at each part of the screen and tells you what it does. Skip it if you like, and replay it any time from the ? button.

Get started

Try Brush X in three steps

  1. Install it. On Android, download the APK, open it, and allow “Install unknown apps” when asked. Or open the web app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Make a project. Tap New Project, pick a size (or a TikTok/YouTube preset) and a frame rate. The beginner guide starts by itself.
  3. Draw, animate, export. Add frames, turn on onion skin, then Export as a video or GIF.
Download for Android Open the web app
How do I install an APK?

Download the file on your phone. Tap it. If Android says it blocks unknown apps, tap Settings and allow your browser or Files app to install apps, then go back and tap Install. Test builds are signed for testing only.

Where are my projects stored?

On your device, inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes them, so use Backup all (Projects screen) or sign in to cloud sync to keep a copy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Drawing, animating and exporting all happen on your device. Only cloud sync needs the internet.

Which browsers work?

Recent Chrome and Edge are best, including video export. Other browsers can draw and export GIF and PNG.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Tell us in the testers’ group or message the developer. Screenshots help a lot.
